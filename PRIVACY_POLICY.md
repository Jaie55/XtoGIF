# XtoGIF Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-14

This Privacy Policy explains how XtoGIF handles information when you use the browser extension.

## Summary

- XtoGIF is designed to download media from X (Twitter) when you explicitly request it.
- XtoGIF does not sell personal data.
- XtoGIF does not use analytics or telemetry services to track your behavior across websites.
- Most extension data is stored locally in your browser.

## Data We Process

XtoGIF may process the following data only to provide extension features:

- Content context on X/Twitter pages needed to detect downloadable media.
- Media URLs and related metadata required to perform user-initiated downloads.
- Cookie/session context from X/Twitter required to access content that your own account is already authorized to view.
- Local settings and records (for example, preferences and local download history) stored in browser extension storage.

XtoGIF is not intended to collect sensitive personal categories such as health data, financial account data, government IDs, or biometric data.

## How We Use Data

Data is used only for extension functionality, including:

- Showing download controls on supported X/Twitter pages.
- Downloading files that you explicitly request.
- Optional local processing for GIF conversion.
- Saving your extension preferences and local operational records.
- Showing notifications about download status and errors.

## Permissions and Why They Are Needed

- downloads: Save files to your device when you click download.
- cookies: Read X/Twitter session context required for authorized content access.
- storage and unlimitedStorage: Store settings and local records in browser storage.
- notifications: Show download/result notifications.
- offscreen: Run background media processing for features such as GIF conversion.
- management (optional): Used only if you run extension-conflict diagnostics manually.
- host permissions for x.com/twitter.com domains: Required to run only on supported sites.

## Data Sharing

- XtoGIF does not sell user data.
- XtoGIF does not transfer user data to third parties for advertising.
- XtoGIF does not transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
- Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

## Data Retention and Control

- Local extension data remains on your device until you remove it.
- You can clear data by uninstalling the extension or clearing extension storage in the browser.

## Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect data processed by the extension. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published in this repository.
