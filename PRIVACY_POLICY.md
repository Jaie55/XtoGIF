# XtoGIF Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-14

XtoGIF does not collect, store, retain, sell, or transfer any personal data.

## Important Statement

- We do not store any user data, locally or remotely.
- We do not use analytics.
- We do not use telemetry.
- We do not maintain user profiles.
- We do not share data with third parties.

## Data Handling

XtoGIF performs user-requested download actions only. No personal data is collected or stored as part of these actions.
